Each color's lightness is tuned so it has the same contrast on white and on the dark background (#0D1117). About 4.3:1 on both is the most any color can reach against those two backgrounds at once, and it is above the 3:1 needed for icons. Palettes differ only in saturation and hue: Vivid is closest to screen RGB, Balanced is calmer, Warm shifts red toward orange and blue toward cyan.
